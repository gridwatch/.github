## Scope

This privacy statement applies to [grdwt.ch](https://grdwt.ch/), the GRIDWATCH applications, and all domains and repositories operated under [@gridwatch](https://github.com/gridwatch).

## What We Collect

GRIDWATCH does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No accounts. No email addresses. No names. No location tracking. No advertising identifiers.

## Analytics

We use [Datadog](https://www.datadoghq.com/) to collect anonymized performance and usage metrics from our servers. These metrics help us understand system health and improve the simulator. They include:

* **Server performance** — response times, error rates, uptime
* **Simulation metrics** — number of sessions played, average turn count, action frequency
* **Battlefield generation** — generation duration, feature counts

These metrics are aggregate and anonymized. They contain no player identifiers, device identifiers, or information that could be used to identify an individual.

## What We Do Not Do

* We do not use cookies on our website
* We do not track you across websites
* We do not serve ads or share data with ad networks
* We do not sell or share data with third parties
* We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or fingerprints for analytics purposes

## On-Device Data

Simulation state, loadout presets, match history, and settings are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage (SwiftData, UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device unless you delete the app.

## Minors

Our services are not directed at minors.

If you are a minor when you use our services, we assume that you have received parental permission beforehand.

## Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data, most data-subject rights do not apply by default. If you believe we hold data about you in error, you may:

* ask us to confirm what, if anything, we hold about you
* ask us to delete anything we hold about you
* submit a complaint about our data practices

## Changes to This Policy

We may amend this privacy statement if necessary.

The latest version can be found at [github.com/gridwatch/.github/blob/main/PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/gridwatch/.github/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).

## Contact

If you have a question about this privacy policy, email `privacy [at] grdwt.ch` and include the word `PRIVACY` in the subject line.
